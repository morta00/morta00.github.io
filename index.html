Kaggle Competition · 1st Place

Multiclass image classification

I trained three CNNs from scratch to classify 16×16 RGB images. The final ensemble reached 93.4% test accuracy and placed first in the competition.

  • Upscaled the inputs to 32×32 before training
  • Used WideResNet-28-10, WideResNet-40-4, and ResNet-110
  • Added CutMix, Mixup, two rounds of pseudo-labeling, and 15-pass TTA
  • Selected the ensemble weights by grid search on the validation set

Healthcare fraud detection

Insurance fraud drives billions in losses every year. I built a cost-sensitive model that flags fraudulent providers while optimizing for money saved — not just raw accuracy.

74 Features
