  • Pipeline: Google Earth Engine scripts that map stress zones to exact coordinates in Michigan parks
  • Result: interactive anomaly maps for 2025 field seasons (Muskegon, Hoffmaster, Lake Forest)

Kaggle Competition · 1st Place

Multiclass image classification

I trained three CNNs from scratch on low-resolution RGB images, then ensembled them to place 1st with 93.4% test accuracy.

  • Upscaled 16×16 inputs to 32×32 before training
  • WideResNet-28-10, WideResNet-40-4, and ResNet-110 for model diversity
  • CutMix, Mixup, two-round pseudo-labeling, and 15-pass TTA
  • Set the ensemble weights with a grid search on the validation split
